About Nefertiti

Nefertiti was one of the most famous queens of ancient Egypt. She was the Great Royal Wife of Pharaoh Akhenaten and lived during the Eighteenth Dynasty. Nefertiti played an important role during her husband's reign and was closely associated with the religious changes of the period. She is especially famous for her beautifully preserved painted limestone bust, which is one of the most recognizable artworks of ancient Egypt.
