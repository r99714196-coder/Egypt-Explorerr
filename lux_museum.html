About Luxor Museum

Luxor Museum is an archaeological museum located on the east bank of the Nile in Luxor, Egypt. It displays a carefully selected collection of ancient Egyptian artifacts, including statues, jewelry, and objects from the temples and tombs of the area. The museum is known for its well-organized exhibitions and its connection to the history of ancient Thebes.
