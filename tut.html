About Tutankhamun

Tutankhamun was an ancient Egyptian pharaoh of the Eighteenth Dynasty. He became king when he was only a child and ruled Egypt around 1332–1323 BC. Although his reign was short, he became one of the world's most famous pharaohs after British archaeologist Howard Carter discovered his nearly untouched tomb in the Valley of the Kings in 1922. The tomb contained thousands of treasures, including the famous golden mask.
