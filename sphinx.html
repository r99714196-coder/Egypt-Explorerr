About the Great Sphinx

The Great Sphinx of Giza is one of Egypt's most famous monuments. It has the body of a lion and the head of a pharaoh, believed to represent King Khafre. The Sphinx was carved from a single piece of limestone over 4,500 years ago. It stands beside the Pyramids of Giza and is a symbol of strength, wisdom, and ancient Egyptian civilization.
