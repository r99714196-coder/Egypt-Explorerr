About Qaitbay Citadel

Qaitbay Citadel is a historic fortress located on the Mediterranean coast in Alexandria. It was built in 1477 by Sultan Al-Ashraf Qaitbay to protect Egypt from naval attacks. The citadel stands on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Today, it is one of Alexandria's most famous tourist attractions.
