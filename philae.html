About Philae Temple

Philae Temple is one of the most beautiful ancient temples in Egypt. It is located on Agilkia Island near Aswan and is dedicated to the goddess Isis. The temple is famous for its elegant architecture, impressive carvings, and fascinating history. After the construction of the Aswan High Dam, the temple was carefully relocated to protect it from flooding.
