About Cleopatra

Cleopatra VII was the last active ruler of the Ptolemaic Kingdom of Egypt. She is one of the most famous women in history, known for her intelligence, political skills, and leadership. Cleopatra spoke several languages and worked to strengthen Egypt through diplomacy and alliances. Her life and reign have inspired countless books, movies, and historical studies.
