About Ramesses II

Ramesses II, also known as Ramesses the Great, was one of the greatest pharaohs of ancient Egypt. He ruled for about 66 years during the Nineteenth Dynasty and is remembered for his military victories, strong leadership, and impressive building projects. He built famous monuments such as Abu Simbel, expanded Karnak and Luxor Temples, and left a lasting legacy in Egyptian history.
