About the Step Pyramid of Djoser

The Step Pyramid of Djoser is the oldest pyramid in Egypt and the first large stone building in history. It was built during the Third Dynasty for Pharaoh Djoser by the famous architect Imhotep. Located in Saqqara, it is considered the beginning of pyramid construction in ancient Egypt and remains one of the country's most important historical landmarks.
