About Al-Azhar Mosque

Al-Azhar Mosque is one of the most famous and historic mosques in Cairo, Egypt. It was founded in the 10th century by the Fatimid dynasty and became an important center of Islamic learning. Today, Al-Azhar is closely associated with Al-Azhar University and remains an important landmark in Islamic Cairo.
