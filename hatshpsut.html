About Hatshepsut Temple

The Temple of Hatshepsut is one of the most beautiful temples in ancient Egypt. It is located at Deir el-Bahari near Luxor and was built for Queen Hatshepsut, one of Egypt's greatest female pharaohs. The temple is famous for its unique terraces, impressive columns, and stunning architecture that blends perfectly with the surrounding cliffs.
