Luxor Temple

About Luxor Temple

Luxor Temple is one of the most famous ancient temples in Egypt. It is located on the east bank of the Nile River in Luxor. The temple was mainly built by Pharaoh Amenhotep III and later expanded by Ramses II. It is known for its magnificent statues, towering columns, and beautiful ancient inscriptions that reflect the greatness of Egyptian civilization.
